# Privacy Policy for Swigaato Mobile Application

**Effective Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026  
**Developer:** Swigaato Technologies, Gonda, Uttar Pradesh, India

Swigaato ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application ("Swigaato") available on the Google Play Store.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Location Data
When using Swigaato, we request permission to access your device's approximate and precise location (GPS and network-based):
- **Purpose**: To determine if your delivery address is within our active Gonda service zones (Civil Lines, Station Road, Bahraich Road, etc.), calculate distance-based delivery fees, and show live delivery partner routes.
- **Background Location**: We do not collect continuous background location from customer devices.

### B. Personal and Contact Information
- **Phone Number**: Used for account creation, secure one-time password (OTP) verification, and delivery-related SMS notifications.
- **Name and Saved Addresses**: Used to label your orders and deliver to designated landmarks in Gonda.

### C. Order and Transaction Details
- Details of meals ordered, restaurant chosen, timestamp, delivery notes, and total bill.

### D. Financial and Payment Information
- All online payments (UPI, Debit/Credit cards, Net Banking) are processed through Reserve Bank of India (RBI) authorized payment aggregators (e.g., Razorpay/PayU) over 256-bit SSL encryption.
- **We never store your card CVV, PIN, or banking passwords on our servers.**

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

1. To process, prepare, and deliver your food orders across Gonda.
2. To coordinate delivery partner dispatches and enable direct communication with your assigned rider.
3. To calculate applicable promotional discounts (e.g., `GONDA50`).
4. To provide prompt customer support via chat or phone.
5. To prevent fraud and enforce Gonda service boundaries.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share your data solely with:
- **Assigned Restaurant Partners**: Only your ordered items and delivery name/notes.
- **Assigned Delivery Riders**: Your delivery address, phone number, and delivery OTP for order fulfillment.
- **Authorized Cloud & Gateway Providers**: Encrypted infrastructure required for SMS OTP dispatch and payment processing.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures, including HTTPS encryption for all in-transit communications, to protect your data against unauthorized access.

---

## 5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to:
- Access and review your order history.
- Update your saved delivery addresses.
- Request account deletion and removal of your personal data by emailing `privacy@swigaato.com`.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Swigaato does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
