## 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 chars)

Swigaato is Gonda's dedicated food delivery platform, connecting you with your favourite local kitchens, sweet houses, and restaurants across Gonda, Uttar Pradesh.

National delivery apps treat smaller cities as an afterthought. Swigaato is built exclusively for Gonda: local flavours, fast delivery inside our city radius, bilingual Hindi + English interface, UPI and Cash on Delivery, and customer support that truly knows Gonda.

### Why Gonda Loves Swigaato:

1. **Gonda's Best Local Restaurants**:
   Order delicious Awadhi Biryani from Station Road, crispy Aloo Tikki and Chaat from Civil Lines, traditional pure veg Thalis from Bahraich Road, and fresh Desi Ghee sweets from Utraula Road.

2. **Ultra-Fast Local Delivery**:
   Our delivery fleet operates inside Gonda's municipal zones (Civil Lines, Station Road, Gandhi Park, Circular Road, Pant Nagar, Malviya Nagar). Expect your meals piping hot in under 30 minutes.

3. **Secure & Flexible Payments**:
   - Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and any UPI ID
   - Debit & Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay)
   - Swigaato Wallet for instant 1-click checkout
   - Cash on Delivery (COD) available across all Gonda areas

4. **Live GPS Order Tracking**:
   Watch your delivery partner ride from the kitchen to your doorstep in real-time. Delivery is confirmed using a secure 4-digit OTP to guarantee your food is handed over safely.

5. **Exciting Offers & Discounts**:
   - Use code **GONDA50** on your first order to get 50% OFF (up to ₹100).
   - Daily restaurant discounts and free delivery coupons.

6. **Dedicated Local Support**:
   Have a question? Reach out through in-app support or phone call. Our support team speaks Hindi & English and understands Gonda's landmarks.
